Our standards

Every story is approved by an editor. Facts are cited, allegations are labelled as allegations, and we publish corrections openly when we get something wrong.

We would rather be right than first.

Why we make our own

We want to use StoryOS ourselves before we ask anyone else to. Making our own stories also shows us which subjects and formats reach people.
