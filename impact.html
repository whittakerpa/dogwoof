Access

Many important stories are told by people working alone, with no research team or lawyer. We build tools for them.

For independent storytellers, wherever they are.

Truth and trust

True stories affect real people. Our tools keep facts and claims apart, show where each one came from, and make it easy to fix mistakes.

Your ownership

Your research and your sources are the value in your work. They stay yours. You can take them with you at any time, and sensitive material can stay on your own computer.

Responsible AI

AI does the routine work. People decide what is published, what is said about real people, what is spent and what is signed.

We say where AI was used. We protect sources. We never train on your work without asking.
