Access

Some of the most important true stories are being told by people with no studio, no research team and no lawyer behind them. We want those storytellers to have the same intelligence and tools as the biggest companies, in every format they work in.

We serve independent storytellers wherever they work, from the USA to Singapore to Vietnam.

Truth and trust

True stories carry real consequences for real people. Our systems never invent a source, a quote or a right. They keep facts, allegations and inferences apart, and they make it easy to correct the record in public when something is wrong.

Creator ownership

A storyteller's research, sources and judgement are their capital. They stay in a record the storyteller owns, in open formats, which they can take with them at any time. Sensitive material can stay on their own machines.

Responsible AI

Agents do the routine work. People decide what gets published, what is said about real people, what is spent and what is signed. We label where AI was used, protect sources and contributors, and never use a creator's material to train anything without their consent.
