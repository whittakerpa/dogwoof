Impact

More people telling true stories.

Great stories come from everywhere. The tools to discover, develop and share them should too.

We’re building AI technology that makes research, knowledge and creative tools more accessible — giving independent storytellers the capabilities once reserved for large organisations.

  • 01
    Access
    Powerful tools. Open to more people. Making advanced AI, research and creative technology accessible to independent storytellers, wherever they are.
  • 02
    Truth & Trust
    Evidence matters. Connecting facts to sources, making research traceable and helping people distinguish evidence from interpretation.
  • 03
    Your Ownership
    Your story. Your data. Your research, knowledge and creative work belong to you. We’re building tools that give you control over how they’re stored, used and shared.
  • 04
    Responsible AI
    AI helps. People decide. We believe in human judgement, transparent methods and responsible use of AI. Technology should expand human creativity, not replace it.

Technology with a purpose.

We’re exploring how AI can make knowledge more accessible, reveal new connections and help people tell stories that might otherwise go unheard.

More voices. Better tools. New possibilities.
