StoryOS

An AI research tool for true stories

StoryOS helps you organise research for a true story. It links each claim to the source it came from, labels how well supported it is, and keeps your research in a record you own.

How it works

  1. SourcesDocuments, archives, interviews and notes, read where they live.
  2. Cited claimsEvery fact traced back to the line it came from, and checked again before it is used.
  3. TimelinePeople, places and events in order, with the gaps made visible.
  4. DossierA research file you can hand to an editor, a lawyer or a commissioner.
  5. Editorial reviewA person reviews and approves the claims. Corrections are kept so they can improve later drafts.
  6. PackageA treatment brief to start development, with every claim still linked to its source.

Truth comes first

It is easy to lose track of what is known and what is guessed. StoryOS keeps them apart: every claim carries one of four labels, and claims must come from the text of a source.

  • Fact
  • Documented assertion
  • Allegation
  • Inference

Allegations stay labelled as allegations until a person decides the evidence supports more.

Your story, your record

Your sources, your research and everything StoryOS learns from your corrections belong to you. Sensitive material can stay on your own machines, data is kept in open formats, and you can take it with you at any time.

If you change AI tools, your record comes with you.
