StoryOS

Research you can trust

True stories start with research. StoryOS helps you keep it in order.

Every fact links back to where it came from. Nothing is guessed. And the research is yours.

How it works

  1. Bring your sourcesDocuments, archives, interviews and notes. They stay where they are.
  2. Find the factsEach fact is linked to the exact line it came from.
  3. See the timelinePeople, places and events in order. You can see what is missing.
  4. You decideA person checks and approves every claim. Nothing is final until you say so.
  5. Share itA clear research file for your editor, lawyer or funder. Every fact still has its source.

Truth comes first

It is easy to mix up what is known and what is guessed. StoryOS keeps them apart. Every claim gets a clear label.

  • Fact
  • Documented assertion
  • Allegation
  • Inference

An allegation stays an allegation until a person decides otherwise.

Your story, your record

Your sources and your research belong to you. Sensitive material can stay on your own computer. You can take everything with you at any time.

Change tools whenever you like. Your record comes with you.
