StoryOS

Develop the story.

A connected workspace for research, writing, production and creative collaboration.

We are starting with research. Every fact links back to where it came from. Nothing is guessed. And the work is yours.

How it works

  1. Sources, notes and cited claimsEvery fact traced back to the line it came from, and checked again before it is used.
  2. TimelinePeople, places and events in order, with the gaps made visible.
  3. DossierA research file you can hand to an editor.
  4. Editorial reviewA person checks and approves every claim.
  5. PackagingEverything ready to develop, with every claim still linked to its source.
  6. StoriesFrom first idea to final story.

Truth comes first

It is easy to mix up what is known and what is guessed. StoryOS keeps them apart. Every claim gets a clear label.

  • Fact
  • Documented assertion
  • Allegation
  • Inference

An allegation stays an allegation until a person decides otherwise.

Your story, your record

Your sources and your research belong to you. Sensitive material can stay on your own computer. You can take everything with you at any time.

Change tools whenever you like. Your record comes with you.
