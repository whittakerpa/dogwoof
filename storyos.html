StoryOS

The AI system for true stories

StoryOS turns a pile of sources into a story you can stand behind. Every fact is cited, every claim is labelled, and your research stays in a record you own.

How it works

  1. SourcesDocuments, archives, interviews and notes, read where they live.
  2. Cited claimsEvery fact traced back to the line it came from, and checked again before it is used.
  3. TimelinePeople, places and events in order, with the gaps made visible.
  4. DossierA research file an editor, a lawyer or a commissioner can trust.
  5. Editorial reviewA person approves what matters. Every correction makes the next draft better.
  6. PackagePitch and a plan for each format, from short video and vertical series to VR, AR and XR experiences.

Truth comes first

General AI tools blur what is known with what is guessed. StoryOS keeps them apart. Every claim carries one of four labels, and nothing is ever invented.

  • Fact
  • Documented assertion
  • Allegation
  • Inference

No made-up quotes, sources or rights. Allegations stay allegations until the evidence says otherwise.

Your story, your record

Your sources, your research and everything StoryOS learns from your corrections belong to you. Sensitive material can stay on your own machines, data is kept in open formats, and you can take it with you at any time.

When better AI models arrive, your record carries over. You never start again from a blank prompt.
