About dogwoof.ai

We are a small team in Singapore, building AI tools for independent storytellers everywhere.

AI should empower storytellers, not replace them.

What we believe

  1. Truth firstIn a true story, trust is everything. Every fact should have a source.
  2. Your story is yoursYour research, your data and your ideas stay with you.
  3. Any formatOne story can be a video, a series, a podcast or something new. You should not have to work it all out alone.
  4. Independent, everywhereMany storytellers work without a studio behind them. We build for them, wherever they are.
  5. Small on purposeWe are a small team. AI helps us do more.

How we work

AI handles the routine work. People handle the judgement.

We write down our decisions and our mistakes, so we can learn from them.

Partners

We also help media and production companies with data and AI, in systems they own.
