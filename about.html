About

A circle, not a pyramid

dogwoof.ai is a technology company, not a production company. It is built around an intelligence layer. AI sits at the centre and does the routine work. People sit at the edge, close to the stories and the storytellers, and make the calls that matter.

What we believe

  1. Truth firstIn true stories, trust is the product. We never invent a source, a quote or a right.
  2. Storytellers own their storyTheir research, their data and what the system learns from them stay theirs.
  3. Every formatOne story can become a short video, a vertical series, a podcast or a VR, AR or XR experience. Creators should not have to work out every format alone.
  4. Asia firstSome of the most exciting storytellers and content creators in the world are in Asia, and the big studios and platforms barely serve them.
  5. Small on purposeA few people with great judgement, working with agents, can do what once took a studio.

How we work

Everyone at dogwoof.ai builds, owns an outcome, and teaches by doing. Agents handle the work we already know how to do. People handle what has not been figured out yet: judging what is good, noticing what has changed, and deciding what should exist next.

Every correction we make is kept, so the company gets better with every story it tells.

Partners

We build data and AI systems for media, production and technology companies, in systems they own, so their knowledge compounds for them rather than for a vendor.
