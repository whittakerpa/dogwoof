About dogwoof.ai

dogwoof.ai is a small technology company in Singapore building AI tools for independent storytellers. We use AI for routine work, and people make the decisions.

What we believe

  1. Truth firstIn true stories, trust matters most. Claims should be traceable to their sources.
  2. Storytellers own their storyTheir research, their data and what the system learns from them stay theirs.
  3. Every formatOne story can become a short video, a vertical series, a podcast or a VR, AR or XR experience. Creators should not have to work out every format alone.
  4. Independent, everywhereStorytellers work everywhere, from Los Angeles to Singapore to Ho Chi Minh City, and many work without a studio behind them. We build for independent storytellers wherever they are.
  5. Small on purposeWe are a small team and use AI to do more with fewer people.

How we work

Each person owns an area of work end to end. AI tools handle routine tasks; people handle judgement, editorial decisions and anything new.

We keep a record of our decisions and corrections so we can learn from them.

Partners

We also work with media and production companies on data and AI projects, in systems they own.
