About Dogwoof AI

Our purpose is stories.

We’re a Singapore-based technology company building AI tools that help people discover, understand, create and share true stories.

We bring together artificial intelligence, research, knowledge systems and creative technology — making powerful capabilities accessible to independent storytellers everywhere.

AI should empower people, not replace them.

What we believe

  1. Truth mattersKnowledge should be traceable. We build around sources, evidence and the ability to distinguish facts from interpretation.
  2. Independence mattersYour research, ideas and creative work should remain under your control. Technology should give people more freedom, not less.
  3. Innovation mattersWe’re exploring new ways to connect information, discover patterns and turn knowledge into stories — across formats and technologies.
  4. People matterAI can analyse, organise and generate. But curiosity, creativity and judgement remain human.

How it fits together

Intelligence discovers. StoryOS connects. Studio delivers. Lab experiments. Originals demonstrates.

  1. Intelligence discoversFinding, researching and verifying true stories.
  2. StoryOS connectsResearch, writing, production and collaboration in one workspace.
  3. Studio deliversPost-production, rights, financing and distribution, with partners.
  4. Lab experimentsDeveloping and testing new methods and technologies.
  5. Originals demonstratesApplying our technology to real stories, producing evidence of what works.
