dogwoof.ai builds technology for independent storytellers

Our purpose is stories. Telling a true story well takes a studio’s worth of work: research, footage, rights, editing and money. We are building AI tools that make that work simpler for independent storytellers, from the first idea to the finished piece. Everything sits in one data lake that the storyteller owns, and their editors and post-production partners work on the same record.
